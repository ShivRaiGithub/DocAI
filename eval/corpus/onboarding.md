# New Hire Onboarding

Your first day starts at 9:00 AM at the main office reception. Collect your laptop from the IT desk on the second floor.

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year, in addition to public holidays.
The probation period lasts 3 months, with a review meeting at the end.

Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase. Late reports may not be reimbursed.
